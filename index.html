Auto-scored math items from natural language

How it works

Describe the item

The author describes the construction and what counts as a correct answer.

Build the figure

LidNote turns that description into an interactive scene.

Let the student interact

Students drag, rotate and manipulate the figure in 2D, 3D, or both.

Check the mathematics

The solver evaluates the interaction against the item's constraints.

A specialist component for your assessment stack

Assessment and courseware platforms license specialized math components like calculators, equation input and subject-specific interactives. LidNote provides the equivalent solution for complex interactive math items.

We handle the complexity
Building in-house means handling a specialized geometry engine, authoring, scoring and compliance infrastructure, indefinitely. We handle this so that assessment platforms can focus on their authors and learners.
Keep your platform focused
Platforms compete on delivery, psychometrics and integrations. The geometry engine is our core business; it improves every quarter without your roadmap having to pay for it.
Maintained for you
Designed for keyboard navigation and screen-reader accessibility, with ongoing attention to conformance, device compatibility and interop.
